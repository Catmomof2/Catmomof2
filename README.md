Author and front-end developer in Lakewood, Washington — I write books about grief, love, and the animals who shape us, and I build practical web apps, usually with a cat nearby.

- Also the author of *Time's Tears*, *The Warden of the Clockwork*, and the poetry and letters collection *Ashes & Ink*

## Featured projects

- [Whisker Haven](https://github.com/Catmomof2/whisker-haven) — An app for adult cat rescue and shelter operations: cat intake and profiles, health and medical tracking, a workflow board from Intake to Medical Hold to Available for Adoption, a daily operations checklist, an adoption application queue, and an AI Adoption Bio Generator for each cat. Built with Vite and Supabase.

- [KDP Launch HQ](https://github.com/Catmomof2/kdp-launch-hq) — A public template for self-publishers: a launch dashboard that saves entries, tracks books, covers, interiors, and upload checklists. Seeded only with fictional sample books.

- [Chronophage Lament](https://chronophage-lament.onrender.com) — A live book site with 12 chapters and an About the Author page.
